02 / About

Designer, artist, & teacher.

My path into UX wasn't traditional. That's my secret weapon.

Before tech, I spent 20+ years as an artist and a decade teaching design. I hold an MFA from Boston University. That background shows up in everything I do: visual craft, a feel for how people learn, and a habit of making complicated things clear.

At Salesforce I designed tools for the people who sell: enterprise platforms, agentic AI, and research that pushed UX upstream into strategy. I love running workshops, partnering closely with engineers, and helping teammates pick up new tools like AI prototyping.
