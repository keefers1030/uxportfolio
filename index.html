Hey, I'm Keith! Thanks for coming to meet me.

I am a Senior UX Designer who sits right at the intersection of complex human problems, business strategy, and emerging technology. My sweet spot is translating high-stakes business requirements into intuitive customer narratives that empower sales teams to win deals, accelerate quota attainment, and drive real organizational growth. Over the past several years, my design work has touched enterprise CRM platforms, agentic AI interfaces, and collaborative tooling, directly contributing to over $110 million in closed revenue.

My path to enterprise UX wasn't traditional, and that is my secret superpower. Before designing for the world's top CRM SaaS company, I spent over 15 years as a cutting-edge design educator in secondary and higher education, along with 20+ years as a fine and commercial artist. Holding a terminal MFA degree from Boston University, I bring a deeply rooted foundation in visual craft, behavioral psychology, and mentorship to every product cycle.

I love the synergy between design and engineering, serving as a translator who bridges development logic with human-centered product vision. Whether facilitating strategy workshops, leveraging AI as a creative partner for rapid prototyping and synthesis, or building scalable design system components, I focus on creating experiences that give users clarity and control.
