Overview

Phone a Friend Agent uses AI to connect sales reps with experts inside the company. When a rep gets stuck or needs specialized knowledge, the agent finds the right person and connects them instantly. It's way faster than searching emails or waiting for calls back.

The challenge was simple: how do we make it easy for people to find the right expert when they need them? And how do we use AI to do that without making it complicated?

The Problem

Sales reps get stuck. They hit a problem and need help from someone who has dealt with it before. The challenge is actually finding that person.

Without a good system, reps waste time searching through emails, company directories, or making calls that nobody picks up. Deals slow down. Opportunities slip away.

What I Did

My job was to take the AI and ML work and turn it into something people actually want to use. Here's how I approached it:

Learn the AI

Understood how the machine learning could match problems to the right experts based on their skills, past work, and background.

Make It Simple

Took all that complex AI logic and turned it into interactions that felt easy and natural to use.

Build Trust

Designed the interface so people understood why the AI was recommending certain experts. That transparency matters.

Test Constantly

Worked with sales teams to make sure the recommendations were actually helpful and the whole thing felt right to use.

How It Works

Smart Matching

The agent looks at the problem and finds experts who have dealt with similar situations. It considers their skills, past projects, and background.

Shows Availability

You can see if the expert is available right now and connect with them quickly. No more back-and-forth emails.

Shows Why

The agent shows a relevance score so you know why it picked that expert. That transparency helps you decide.

Gets Better Over Time

The more people use it, the smarter it gets. Every match teaches it something new.

What I Learned

Good AI product design isn't about showing off fancy technology. It's about solving a real human problem in a way that feels natural.

The best AI products? You don't even think about the AI. It just works. You get what you need and life is easier.
