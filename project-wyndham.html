Experience design · Storytelling

The future-vision story behind a hospitality win

Wyndham runs 9,000+ hotels across 20 brands, and its corporate teams and franchisees were working in disconnected systems. Our team had to show them a better future, in a competitive pitch.

My role

Experience designer

Team

Five-person Q Branch team

What I made

Hero workshop, microsite, and vision story

Outcome

Contract won

9,000+hotels in the Wyndham portfolio
20brands under one roof
4personas carried the story
The challenge

A pitch had to feel like their future, not our features.

Wyndham's systems didn't work well together. Corporate teams and hotel owners couldn't collaborate easily, and other vendors were pitching too. A nice slide deck wouldn't win this. They needed to see how it would work in their world.

My role

Experience designer on a team of five.

I worked alongside lead creative strategists, a lead technical strategist, and a lead experience designer. My part:

  • Hero Workshop: Helped run the engagement workshop with Wyndham's team.
  • Microsite: Designed the microsite that became the hub for the whole engagement.
  • Vision story: Designed the future-vision presentation, about 47 slides.
  • UI advisement: Shaped the demo screens and joined solution meetings.
The story

How might we use a connected guest journey to inspire brand champions?

The vision story had three chapters: streamline the booking experience, reduce operational costs, and enable a world-class loyalty team. Four personas carried it, with live demo moments along the way:

Paola

A Wyndham Rewards member booking and managing her stay.

Leona

A marketing and sales executive.

Terry

A contact center agent.

Brett

A franchise owner.

The outcome

Contract won.

Wyndham signed, and Salesforce's footprint grew to include the Wyndham Rewards Call Center. Over time, the account tracked strong results from the work this engagement set in motion:

21% faster

service conversations, year over year

25% fewer

calls into the contact center

20%

of franchisee inquiries resolved automatically

5% lift

in Wyndham Rewards enrollment

What I learned

Business decisions are human decisions.

Wyndham needed a solution, sure. But what made them sign was that they saw themselves in our story. This is where I found my voice as a facilitator.
