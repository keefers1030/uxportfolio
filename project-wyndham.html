The Problem

Wyndham runs 9,000+ hotels across 20 brands worldwide. They had a big problem: their systems didn't work well together. Corporate teams and hotel owners couldn't collaborate effectively. Decisions were slow. They couldn't optimize revenue.

There were other companies pitching the same kind of solution. To win, we couldn't just make a nice presentation. We had to show them how it would actually work in their world.

Designing for the global Wyndham portfolio: 9,000+ properties across 20 brands

What I Did

I owned the whole customer experience. Not just slides and presentations. The entire strategy:

  • Discovery — Talked to 8+ key people to understand how they work, what problems they face, what success looks like
  • Story Building — Built a narrative that went from problem to solution to outcomes. Every piece served that story.
  • Demos — Created walkthroughs showing how the solution would actually work for different roles: hotel managers, finance teams, executives
  • Visuals — Made complex features easy to understand through design
  • Follow-up Materials — Designed everything after the pitch to show ROI and next steps

How I Did It

I designed every part of how Wyndham would experience our pitch. Presentations, demos, follow-ups. Everything had a purpose.

1. Real Discovery

I didn't just ask questions. I mapped how they actually work. How their finance teams track money. How different brands operate. Where the system breaks down. This shaped everything that came next.

2. Outcome Focus

I built the whole pitch around real outcomes: How much time would managers save? How fast would finance close? What could the CFO actually see that they can't now? Every demo answered: "What's better for them?"

3. Designed for Different People

I created different demos for different roles. One for hotel managers (speed and efficiency). One for finance (visibility and control). One for executives (money and risk). Made each person feel like we understood them.

4. Made Complex Things Clear

Enterprise features are complicated. I translated them into clear stories. Used their language and context. This was about clarity, not pretty design.

What I Designed

40+ Slide Presentation — Told Wyndham's transformation story. Not just features, but how their business changes.

Custom Demos — Walkthroughs for different roles showing how each person would actually use the system.

Follow-up Materials — ROI projections, implementation plan, change strategy. Everything they needed to feel confident moving forward.

Why This Matters

This shows that strategic design goes beyond apps and websites. I can:

  • Understand complex businesses — Getting a $2B+ global company takes empathy and business sense
  • Design experiences that persuade — Moved them from doubtful to committed through strategy and storytelling
  • Deliver business results — This won a deal. That's the real measure of success.
  • Work at enterprise level — Shaped strategy with sales, delivery, and leadership teams

Scope of experience design work spanning personalized messaging, in-app features, and customer engagement strategies

What I Learned

Business decisions are human decisions. Wyndham needed a solution, sure. But what made them sign was that they saw themselves in our story. They understood how their people would use it. They felt good about moving forward.

Design works everywhere. The principles that make great products also work at enterprise level: understand your user, be clear about what you're doing, think about how people experience things. It doesn't matter if it's an app or a business deal.

Real impact matters most. This wasn't about winning design awards. It was about winning a deal that would improve operations for thousands of hotels and create value for everyone involved.
